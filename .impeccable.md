## Design Context

### Users
People browsing social and content-heavy websites who want to share one visible card, post, or panel without opening a separate screenshot editor or manually dragging a crop box.

### Brand Personality
Focused, quick, quietly confident. The product should feel like a small, well-made developer tool: immediate feedback, no ceremony, and clear control.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined utilitarian UI with warm paper neutrals and a sharp coral capture accent. Light-first, compact, and typographically expressive without looking like a generic dashboard.

### Design Principles
1. Make the one-click capture action obvious and fast.
2. Show spatial feedback directly on the webpage, close to the user's cursor.
3. Keep controls minimal; expose detail only when it helps the next action.
4. Use restrained contrast and one memorable accent to signal selection state.
